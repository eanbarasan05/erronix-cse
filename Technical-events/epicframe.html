About the Event

Epic Frames is a creative wireframing competition where participants showcase their UI/UX design skills by creating wireframes for an on-the-spot topic. The contest emphasizes problem-solving, clarity, usability, and innovation in design, testing participants’ ability to convert abstract ideas into effective digital blueprints.

Event Schedule

  • Date: 31th October 2025
  • Time: 11.00 PM to 12.00 PM
  • Venue: Will be updated soon

Category & Participation

  • Category/Theme: UI/UX Design, Wireframing
  • Participation: Individual (first-come-first-serve)

Registration & Prerequisites

  • Registration Process: Online / First-come, first-served (No on spot registration).
  • Registration Deadline: 29/10/2025.
  • Prerequisite: Valid college ID card for verification

Event Rules & Guidelines

General Rules:

  • Must carry a valid college ID card.
  • Only original work is allowed; plagiarism will lead to disqualification.
  • Judges’ decisions are final.
  • Maintain decorum throughout the event.

Specific Rules:

  • Design tool: Figma (no plugins, AI tools, or external templates allowed).
  • Topic/question will be given on the spot (same for all participants).
  • Wireframe must solve or support the scenario/problem described in the question.
  • Participants must use only the Gmail account provided on the event system for Figma access.
  • Design must be for mobile view only (not desktop or tablet).
  • Page Limit: Minimum 2 pages, maximum 3 pages.
  • Do NOT use sample templates or copy designs from other sources.
  • No preview designs will be provided; design must be created from scratch.
  • All submissions must be shared via the Google Form provided during the event.
  • Electronic devices (smartphones, smartwatches, etc.) are strictly prohibited unless part of the event system.

Evaluation Criteria:

  • How well the design meets the given problem and requirements.
  • Creativity, innovation, and overall presentation of the wireframe.
  • Clarity, consistency, and usability of the design flow between screens.
  • Visual aesthetics, alignment, spacing, and overall design quality.

Submission:

  • Final Figma file link must be submitted using the Google Form shared with participants.
  • Ensure your Figma design uses only the Gmail account provided on the event system.

Event Flow & Time Allocation

  • Design Time: 40–45 minutes
  • Evaluation Time: 15 minutes
  • Number of Rounds: Single round (on-the-spot wireframe design)

Rewards & Recognition

  • All participants will receive a Participation Certificate.
  • Winners and Runners-up will be awarded special prizes in recognition of their outstanding performance.

Code of Conduct

  • Be punctual and arrive 10 minutes before the event.
  • Follow instructions given by coordinators.
  • Any misbehaviour, cheating, or violation of rules will lead to disqualification.
