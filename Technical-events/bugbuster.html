BugBuster

CODE DEBUGGING CONTEST

About the Event

BugBuster is a challenging debugging competition that tests programming and problem-solving skills. Participants will be given code snippets containing errors which they must identify and correct, demonstrating logical thinking, coding precision, and efficiency.

Event Timing & Venue

  • Date: 31th October 2025
  • Time: 11.00 AM to 12.00 PM
  • Venue: Will be updated soon

Category & Participation

  • Category/Theme: Programming / Problem-Solving
  • Participation: Individual only

Registration & Submission

  • Registration Process: Online / First-come, first-served.
  • Registration Deadline: 29/10/2025.
  • Prerequisite: No prior submission required.

General Rules

  1. Only individual participation is allowed.
  2. Participants must use original effort; malpractice leads to disqualification.
  3. The decision of the judges/organizers will be final.

Specific Rules

  1. Each participant will receive a set of debugging questions covering C, Java, and Python.
  2. All participants must attempt questions from all three languages within the given time limit of 40 minutes.
  3. Each question will contain syntax and/or logical errors that participants must identify and correct.
  4. Internet usage is strictly prohibited during the event.
  5. Only the allocated systems must be used.
  6. Participants must ensure the corrected programs produce the expected output before submission.
  7. No gadgets to be brought inside.

DON’Ts:

  1. No cheating or malpractice will be tolerated.
  2. Do not discuss questions or answers with other participants.
  3. Electronic devices (smartphones, smartwatches, etc.) are strictly prohibited.
  4. Avoid any behavior that could be considered unfair or against the rules.
  5. Respect the judges’ and officials’ decisions; disputes are not allowed.

Event Flow & Time Allocation

  • Total Duration: 40–45 minutes.
  • Format: Single round.

Evaluation Criteria

  • Number of bugs correctly fixed.
  • Time taken to fix the bugs.
  • Ranking is based on more bugs fixation in less time.

Rewards & Recognition

  • All participants will receive a Participation Certificate.
  • Winners and Runners-up will be awarded special prizes in recognition of their outstanding performance.

Code of Conduct

  • Participants must report on time.
  • Maintain professionalism and decorum throughout the event.
  • Any violation of rules may lead to disqualification.
