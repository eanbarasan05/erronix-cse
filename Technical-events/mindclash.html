About the Event

Mindclash is a technical quiz designed to test knowledge, analytical skills, and quick thinking of Computer Science enthusiasts. The event consists of multiple rounds with increasing difficulty, covering core computer science topics and emerging technologies.

Time & Venue

  • Date: 31/10/2025
  • Time: 12.00 PM to 1.00 PM
  • Venue: Will be updated soon

Category & Participation

  • Category/Theme: Computer Science & Engineering (CSE)
  • Participation: Individual (Solo)

Registration & Prerequisites

  • Registration Process: Online
  • Prerequisite: Registration must be completed before the deadline on 29/10/2025.
  • Participants must arrive at the venue at least 10 minutes before the start time
  • The quiz will be conducted online

Event Overview

  • The quiz is divided into three rounds with increasing difficulty.
  • Participants are expected to manage their time effectively and answer accurately.

General Rules & Guidelines

DOs:

  1. Arrive at the quiz venue before the scheduled time.
  2. Carefully read and understand the quiz rules and format.
  3. Participants are required to complete the quiz in a single browser window/tab without switching to other tabs or windows during the quiz.
  4. Any attempt to switch tabs or windows will result in immediate disqualification from the quiz.
  5. A countdown timer will be displayed on the quiz page, and participants must submit their answers before the timer expires.
  6. Failure to submit answers before the timer expires will result in the quiz being automatically submitted with any unanswered questions marked as "Not Attempted".
  7. In the event of any technical issues or disruptions during the quiz, participants must immediately notify the student co-ordinators to seek assistance.

DON’Ts:

  1. No cheating or malpractice will be tolerated.
  2. Do not discuss questions or answers with other participants.
  3. Electronic devices (smartphones, smartwatches, etc.) are strictly prohibited inside the quiz venue.
  4. Avoid any behavior that could be considered unfair or against the rules.
  5. Respect the judges’ and officials’ decisions; disputes are not allowed.

Event Flow & Time Allocation

  • Round 1 – Elimination: 20 Easy Questions
  • Round 2 – Elimination: 15 Medium Questions
  • Round 3 – Winner Selection: 10 Hard Questions

Evaluation Criteria

  • Correctness of answers
  • Accuracy in coding/problem-solving (Round 3)

Rewards & Recognition

  • All participants will receive a Participation Certificate.
  • Winners and Runners-up will be awarded special prizes in recognition of their outstanding performance.

Code of Conduct

  • Participants must follow all rules strictly.
  • Misbehaviour, disputes, or violations may lead to disqualification.
  • Maintain professionalism and decorum during the quiz.
