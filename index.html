Registration Protocol

Important instructions for all participants attending Erronix 2K26

  • ✓
    Read event rules carefully and check timings before registering.
  • ✓
    Register in advance and confirm your attendance online.
  • ✓
    Be punctual for all sessions and competitive event slots.
  • ✓
    Maintain respectful conduct with coordinators, speakers, and participants.
  • ✓
    Adhere strictly to your allotted time if presenting or performing.
  • ✓
    Follow professional dress code throughout the event campus.
  • ✓
    Carry and display your official college ID card at all times.
  • ✓
    Follow instructions given by event coordinators and student volunteers.
  • ✓
    Avoid unnecessary phone calls; use official email for queries.
